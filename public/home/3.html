/// NER CHARTER & PHILOSOPHY

Rooted in Nagpur.
Made for the Journey.

Built around shared roads, lasting friendships, and the unfiltered freedom to explore. In 2018, seven riders met by the Zero Mile sandstone pillar with nothing more than an urge to ride without club bureaucracy.

Today, Nagpur Enfield Riders stands as Central India’s premier touring collective. We do not care what badge year you ride or how shiny your chrome looks. If you respect the formation, carry your own weight, and look out for your pack, you have a place with us.

“The map doesn’t care about your job title. Out on the highway, we are all just listeners to the engine.”

— NER Founding Charter // 2018
