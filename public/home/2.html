THE MANIFESTO

Rooted in Nagpur.
Made for the Journey.

“Built around open tarmac, shared fuel stops, and the unfiltered freedom to ride without bureaucratic pretension.”

In 2018, seven riders gathered by Nagpur’s British Zero Mile sandstone pillar with nothing more than an urge to ride together in disciplined formation. Today, Nagpur Enfield Riders has grown into Central India’s most respected touring fraternity.

“The map doesn’t care about your corporate rank. Out on the highway, we are all just listeners to the engine.”

— NER Founding Charter
