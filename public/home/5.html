Lead & Sweep Mechanical Support

Every guild ride includes two dedicated sweep mechanics carrying spare clutch cables, tube repair kits, fuses, and throttle linkages. No rider is left behind.

forest

Forest & Wildlife Protocol

We coordinate with Maharashtra & MP forest departments. Strict noise discipline, zero littering, and night convoy protocols in tiger corridor buffer belts.

volunteer_activism

Local Highway Solidarity

We patronize small rural chai stalls, repair shops, and family dhabas along our scouting corridors. The road gives back to the towns we pass through.

The Guild Manifesto

One Community.
Countless Unwritten Miles.

Born in Nagpur, NER brings together riders from all walks of life through the roads we explore, the friendships we build, and the quiet moments that stay with us long after the cylinder head cools down.

Whether you ride a 1982 Cast Iron Bullet 350 with the right-foot gearshift or a twin-cylinder Interceptor 650, the tarmac measures only camaraderie and respect.
